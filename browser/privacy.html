▮ Black Mirror Browser — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-19 · kinoshita studio

Black Mirror Browser (the "Extension") is a Chrome extension by kinoshita studio. It opens BMBoard (an infinite‑canvas blackboard at bmboard.studio) as a browser side panel, and adds a right‑click menu to send images and text to the board. This page explains what data the Extension touches.

Short version

The Extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no analytics, no tracking, no account, no server run by us.

What is stored, and where

  • Nothing is stored by the Extension itself. It uses no local storage, no cookies, and no browser sync.
  • Images / text you send to BMBoard (via the right‑click menu) — the Extension fetches the image or selected text and passes it to your BMBoard side panel. It is not sent to us or to any third party.

Network requests the Extension makes

  • bmboard.studio — the side panel loads bmboard.studio/app.html in a frame, and a bridge script runs on that page to receive content you send. BMBoard's own behaviour applies inside that frame; the Extension just hosts it.
  • Images you choose to send — when you right‑click an image and choose "Send image to BMBoard", the Extension fetches that image from the page so it can hand it to your board. No image data is sent to us.

Permissions, and why

  • sidePanel — to show BMBoard alongside any page.
  • contextMenus — to add the "Send image / text to BMBoard" right‑click items.

Third parties

The only external service the Extension contacts is bmboard.studio — to display the board in the side panel and to run the bridge that receives content you send. No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking services. BMBoard's own privacy policy applies to your use of the board itself.

Children

The Extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be noted in the extension's store listing.
